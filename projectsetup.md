Build a Premium Handmade Gift E-commerce Website
Project Overview

Design and develop a modern, elegant, and highly conversion-focused website for a handmade gift brand specializing in crochet products, candles, gift bundles, and customizable handmade gifts.

The website's primary objective is to showcase products beautifully, generate customer inquiries, and establish trust. This is not an online payment store in the first phase. Customers browse products, build an inquiry cart, and submit their inquiry via a contact form or WhatsApp.

The overall design should feel premium, warm, handcrafted, minimal, and visually rich. The products should always be the center of attention.

Visual Experience

The website should feel like browsing a premium handcrafted gift boutique.

Products should dominate the screen.

Use generous spacing and large imagery.

Cards should have subtle hover effects.

Scrolling should feel smooth and elegant.

Avoid unnecessary decorative elements that distract from the products.

Overall Impression

Create a website that feels premium, timeless, trustworthy, and handcrafted. Every design decision should guide visitors toward exploring products, discovering gift ideas, and submitting an inquiry. The experience should inspire confidence and make the products the hero of every page while remaining scalable as the business grows.
